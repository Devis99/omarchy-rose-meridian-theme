# Wallpaper sources

All ten wallpapers are AI-generated with Codex's image generation in a flat vector poster style, then
cleaned and upscaled to 3840x2160: the originals' faint grain and compression blocks are
removed with an edge-preserving filter, edges and detail come from Upscayl's UltraMix Balanced
model, and flat areas from a smooth upscale. Large flat skies are then filled with one exact
base colour (real glows kept), so there are no blotches or banding lines and no added noise.
Saved as lossless PNG.
